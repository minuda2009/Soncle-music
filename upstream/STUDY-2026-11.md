# Upstream study: Metrolist (current) vs Soncle — 24 Nov 2026

Read-only study of MetrolistGroup/Metrolist at `af3d053` (2026-11-24, v14.0.1 released 2026-11-19):
README, `docs/changelog.md`, `docs/innertube.md`, `playback.md`, `audio-processing.md`,
`device-connect.md` and the settings registries. Nothing was copied and no one was contacted.
Metrolist 14 is now a Kotlin Multiplatform app (Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, Linux) on
InnerTubeX v0.13.1 with SABR streaming. What Soncle takes from this is in ROADMAP.md (items 10–13
and the hybrid plan's "later" list).

## Where Metrolist is weak (from its own changelog and code)

- **Desktop is new and still settling.** 14.0.1 had to fix Linux window crashes and audio output,
  cut desktop CPU use, and only now added a system-tray setting and the song in the window title.
- **Stability churn on Android.** Recent releases fix black screens, startup crashes, playback
  freezes, foreground-service ANRs, sleep-timer crashes, login/logout and backup restore.
- **Phone-first UI everywhere.** One Compose UI scaled to tablets and desktop ("automatic tablet UI
  scaling", "improved landscape scaling"), not a desktop-designed layout.
- **Settings sprawl.** About 115 settings source files; many features, little curation.
- **Sound quality is not the focus.** Normalization levels but no true-peak limiter or loudness
  target; AutoEQ by import only.
